## 1. Background

1.1. RNL runs the ticketing and sales for the Partner's events, through its own
official channels.

1.2. This Agreement sets out that arrangement, how the money from each sale is
divided, and how the Partner is paid.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **"Tickets"** means admission to the Partner's events sold under this Agreement.
- **"Packages"** means bundles or upgrades sold alongside a Ticket — for example,
  VIP or add-on packages.
- **"Official Channels"** means RNL's ticketing storefront and any other sales
  channel RNL operates or authorises.
- **"Sale Price"** means the price a buyer pays for a Ticket or Package, in Robux
  (R$).
- **"Roblox Tax"** means the platform fee Roblox deducts on a sale, being **30%**
  of the Sale Price at the date of this Agreement.
- **"Post-Tax Profit"** means the amount RNL receives for a sale after deduction
  of Roblox Tax — that is, **70%** of the Sale Price at the current rate of Roblox
  Tax.
- **"RNL Share"** means **10% of the Post-Tax Profit**.
- **"Partner Share"** means the remaining **90% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

## 3. Sold through RNL

3.1. All Tickets and Packages for the Partner's events are sold **exclusively**
through the Official Channels.

3.2. While this Agreement is in force, the Partner does not sell, resell or issue
Tickets or Packages for its events outside the Official Channels, and does not
authorise anyone else to do so.

3.3. RNL is responsible for issuing, delivering and validating Tickets and
Packages through the Official Channels.

## 4. The division of money

4.1. On each sale, Roblox deducts the Roblox Tax. What remains is the Post-Tax
Profit.

4.2. RNL retains the RNL Share — **10% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

4.3. The Partner receives the Partner Share — **90% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

4.4. **Worked example.** On a Ticket sold for R$100:

| Step | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Sale Price | R$100 |
| less Roblox Tax (30%) | −R$30 |
| **Post-Tax Profit** | **R$70** |
| RNL Share (10% of R$70) | R$7 |
| **Partner Share (90% of R$70)** | **R$63** |

4.5. If Roblox changes its tax rate, "Post-Tax Profit" is calculated using the
rate in force at the time of the sale. The 10% / 90% split of the Post-Tax Profit
is unchanged.

## 5. Payment

5.1. RNL holds and keeps a record of the Partner Share as it accrues.

5.2. The Partner is paid the Partner Share **in full, each month**, for the sales
in the preceding month.

5.3. Payment is made in Robux (R$) to the account the Partner nominates, or by
another method the parties agree in writing.

5.4. RNL provides the Partner with a monthly statement showing the sales, the
Post-Tax Profit, and the Partner Share for the period. Where a sale is refunded or
reversed, the corresponding shares are adjusted in the statement.

## 6. Term and ending this Agreement

6.1. This Agreement starts on the Effective date and continues until it is ended.

6.2. Either party may end this Agreement by giving the other reasonable notice in
writing.

6.3. On ending, RNL stops selling new Tickets and Packages for the Partner's
events. Tickets and Packages already sold remain valid for the events they were
sold for, and any Partner Share already earned but not yet paid remains payable and
is paid under clause 5.

## 7. General

7.1. Any change to this Agreement must be agreed by both parties in writing.

7.2. This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties on the ticketing and
packages for the Partner's events, and replaces any earlier understanding on that
subject.

7.3. This document is a plain-language commercial agreement. It is not legal or tax
advice, and each party is responsible for its own tax affairs.
