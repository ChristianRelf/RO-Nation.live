## 1. Background

1.1. The Partner produces and owns merchandise that it wishes to make available
for sale.

1.2. RNL operates a storefront and official sales channels within Roblox, and is
willing to sell that merchandise on the Partner's behalf.

1.3. This Agreement sets out the terms on which RNL may do so, and how the money
from each sale is divided between the parties.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **"Merchandise"** means the items the Partner makes available for sale under
  this Agreement, together with their designs and artwork.
- **"Official Channels"** means RNL's storefront and any other sales channel RNL
  operates or authorises.
- **"Sale Price"** means the price a buyer pays for an item of Merchandise, in
  Robux (R$).
- **"Roblox Tax"** means the platform fee Roblox deducts on a sale, being **30%**
  of the Sale Price at the date of this Agreement.
- **"Post-Tax Profit"** means the amount RNL receives for a sale after deduction
  of Roblox Tax — that is, **70%** of the Sale Price at the current rate of Roblox
  Tax.
- **"RNL Share"** means **10% of the Post-Tax Profit**.
- **"Partner Share"** means the remaining **90% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

## 3. Permission to sell

3.1. The Partner grants RNL permission to list, present, market and sell the
Merchandise through the Official Channels.

3.2. This permission is **non-exclusive**. The Partner remains free to sell its
Merchandise elsewhere.

3.3. RNL decides how the Merchandise is presented within the Official Channels.
The Partner supplies the Merchandise, its designs and any assets needed to list
it (subject to the separate Asset Usage Agreement).

## 4. The division of money

4.1. On each sale, Roblox deducts the Roblox Tax. What remains is the Post-Tax
Profit.

4.2. RNL retains the RNL Share — **10% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

4.3. The Partner receives the Partner Share — **90% of the Post-Tax Profit**.

4.4. **Worked example.** On a sale of R$100:

| Step | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Sale Price | R$100 |
| less Roblox Tax (30%) | −R$30 |
| **Post-Tax Profit** | **R$70** |
| RNL Share (10% of R$70) | R$7 |
| **Partner Share (90% of R$70)** | **R$63** |

4.5. If Roblox changes its tax rate, "Post-Tax Profit" is calculated using the
rate in force at the time of the sale. The 10% / 90% split of the Post-Tax Profit
is unchanged.

## 5. Payment

5.1. RNL holds and keeps a record of the Partner Share as it accrues.

5.2. The Partner is paid the Partner Share **in full**, at the Partner's choice of
either:

- **on request** — at any time; or
- **automatically each month**.

5.3. Payment is made in Robux (R$) to the account the Partner nominates, or by
another method the parties agree in writing.

5.4. RNL provides the Partner with a statement showing the sales, the Post-Tax
Profit, and the Partner Share for the period.

## 6. Ownership

6.1. The Partner remains the owner of the Merchandise and its designs. This
Agreement is a permission to sell, not a transfer of ownership.

6.2. For the sole purpose of selling the Merchandise under this Agreement, the
Partner grants RNL a limited, non-transferable, revocable licence to reproduce and
display the Merchandise and its designs within the Official Channels.

## 7. Term and ending this Agreement

7.1. This Agreement starts on the Effective date and continues until it is ended.

7.2. Either party may end this Agreement by giving the other reasonable notice in
writing.

7.3. On ending, RNL stops listing the Merchandise. Any Partner Share already
earned but not yet paid remains payable and is paid under clause 5.

## 8. General

8.1. Any change to this Agreement must be agreed by both parties in writing.

8.2. This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties on the selling of
the Merchandise, and replaces any earlier understanding on that subject.

8.3. This document is a plain-language commercial agreement. It is not legal or
tax advice, and each party is responsible for its own tax affairs.
