## 1. Background

1.1. In working together, RNL needs to use some of the Partner's assets — to
promote, stage and sell the Partner's shows, merchandise and packages.

1.2. The Partner owns those assets and is willing to let RNL use them for that
purpose, within reason.

1.3. This Agreement sets out what RNL may use, what it may use it for, and the
limits on that use.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **"Assets"** means the materials the Partner owns or controls and makes
  available to RNL — including logos, artwork, 3D models, audio, brand names and
  other brand materials.
- **"Permitted Purpose"** means promoting, staging and selling the Partner's
  shows, merchandise and packages through RNL and its official channels.

## 3. What RNL may request

3.1. RNL may request the use of the Partner's Assets for the Permitted Purpose.

3.2. A request identifies the Asset and what it is to be used for. The Partner may
grant or decline it.

3.3. RNL uses only the Assets the Partner has made available. It does not take or
use an Asset the Partner has not agreed to.

## 4. Within reason — the limits on use

4.1. RNL uses the Assets only for the Permitted Purpose.

4.2. RNL does **not**:

- sell an Asset, or license it to anyone else, on its own;
- alter an Asset beyond what is needed to present it (for example, resizing or
  placing it in a layout), without the Partner's agreement; or
- use an Asset for anything unrelated to the partnership without the Partner's
  separate agreement.

4.3. RNL uses the Assets in a way that is consistent with the Partner's brand, and
does not bring the Partner into disrepute.

## 5. Ownership and licence

5.1. The Partner keeps ownership of every Asset. Nothing in this Agreement
transfers ownership to RNL.

5.2. The Partner grants RNL a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable and
**revocable** licence to use the Assets for the Permitted Purpose, and for nothing
more.

## 6. The Partner keeps control

6.1. The Partner may decline any specific request without giving a reason.

6.2. The Partner may withdraw an Asset from use at any time, on reasonable notice.
On withdrawal, RNL stops using that Asset as soon as it reasonably can, and
removes it from anything still within RNL's control.

6.3. Withdrawing an Asset does not, on its own, require RNL to recall material
already published to third parties (for example, a post already shared), but RNL
does not continue to publish it anew.

## 7. Credit

7.1. Where an Asset is used publicly, RNL credits the Partner, unless the parties
agree otherwise for a particular use.

## 8. Term and ending this Agreement

8.1. This Agreement starts on the Effective date and continues until it is ended.

8.2. Either party may end this Agreement by giving the other reasonable notice in
writing.

8.3. On ending, the licence in clause 5 ends, and RNL stops using the Assets under
clause 6.2.

## 9. General

9.1. Any change to this Agreement must be agreed by both parties in writing.

9.2. This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties on the use of the
Assets, and replaces any earlier understanding on that subject.

9.3. This document is a plain-language commercial agreement. It is not legal
advice.
